Pediatric Dentistry

A child's first dental visit should take place from the eruption of the first tooth, or at the latest by age 1. Early exposure helps prevent the child from developing a fear of the dentist.

Baby teeth guide jaw development until permanent teeth come in, so untreated cavities can lead to serious problems; the idea that "it will fall out anyway" is a misconception.

Fissure sealants are a painless protective coating applied to the chewing surfaces of molars to prevent cavities. They are typically recommended around ages 6-7, when the permanent molars come in.

Fluoride application strengthens tooth enamel and increases resistance to cavities; it can be applied every 6 months during routine checkups.
