Root Canal Treatment Information

Root canal treatment is performed when the nerve tissue inside a tooth (the pulp) becomes inflamed or dies. The most common causes are deep cavities, repeated dental procedures, or trauma to the tooth.

The treatment process is usually completed in 1-2 sessions. In the first session, local anesthesia is applied, the infected tissue is removed, the canals are shaped and disinfected. In the second session, the canals are permanently filled.

Patients do not feel pain during the procedure because the area is fully numbed. Mild sensitivity for 1-2 days after the procedure is normal; prescribed pain relievers provide comfort during this period.

Teeth that have undergone root canal treatment can become brittle, so a crown is usually recommended afterward. This requires an additional appointment and cost, which our dentist will assess individually during your examination.
