Patient Policies

Appointment Cancellation: If you need to cancel your appointment, please notify us at least 24 hours in advance. Cancellations with less than 24 hours' notice, or missed appointments without notice, may require prepayment for your next appointment.

Payment Options: Cash, credit card, and installment payments are accepted. Installment plans of up to 6 months are available for major treatments (implants, orthodontics).

Insurance: We do not have an agreement with the national health insurance system. We work with most private health insurance providers (including Allianz, Axa, and Groupama); please let us know your insurance provider's name so we can check your coverage.

Lateness: If you are more than 15 minutes late for your appointment, it may need to be rescheduled, as this affects the appointment time of our other patients.

Emergencies: In case of severe toothache, swelling, or trauma, you can request a same-day appointment by calling us during business hours.
