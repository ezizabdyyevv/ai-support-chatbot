Dental Implant Process

A dental implant is a procedure that replaces a missing tooth with an artificial tooth root made of titanium. The process consists of several stages and can take a total of 3-6 months.

In the first stage, the implant screw is surgically placed into the jawbone. This procedure is performed under local anesthesia and usually takes 30-60 minutes.

In the second stage, the implant is allowed to fuse with the jawbone (osseointegration). This process can take 2-3 months in the lower jaw and 3-6 months in the upper jaw, since the upper jawbone is softer.

Once fusion is complete, an artificial tooth (crown) is attached to the implant. This final stage is completed in 1-2 sessions.

Aftercare for implants is simple: normal brushing and flossing are sufficient. Since smoking negatively affects the healing process, it is recommended to reduce or avoid it before and after the implant procedure.
